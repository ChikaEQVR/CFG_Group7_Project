# CFGdegree Data Science Group 7 Project

Title : 
Is there a relationship between fertility rates vs income and house prices? Will this impact how banks target people aged 25-29?
Using data between years 2020 to 2024: What are the potential implications for future demand for financial products and services?



## Project Description
This project investigates whether there is a relationship between:
- Fertility rates among 25–29-year-olds
- Income 
- House prices
Using raw data from years 2020 - 2024 and age group 25 - 29 years: The analysis explores potential implications for financial institutions, mortgage lenders, and policymakers.

## Research Questions
- How have birth rates among 25–29-year-olds changed over time?
- Is housing affordability associated with birth rates?
- Is income associated with birth rates?
- Which factor has the stronger relationship with birth rates?
